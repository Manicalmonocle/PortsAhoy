Privacy Policy — Ports Ahoy!

Last updated: 15 August 2026

Ports Ahoy! collects no data whatsoever. Nothing about you or your device is gathered, stored on a server, or transmitted anywhere.

What the game stores

One save file, held in ordinary application storage on your own device. It contains only the state of your port — buildings, resources, the day number. It never leaves the device. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data, deletes it permanently. There is no cloud save and no account.

What the game does not do

  • No advertising, and no advertising identifier is read.
  • No analytics, telemetry, or crash reporting.
  • No in-app purchases and no payment handling.
  • No accounts, sign-in, or email collection.
  • No location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, or files.
  • No network requests of any kind. The game works fully offline.
  • No third-party SDKs that collect data.

Permissions

The app requests one permission, an internal Android broadcast permission required by the Flutter framework it is built on. It grants no access to anything on your device and exists only so the app can talk to itself.

Children

Because no data is collected from anyone, none is collected from children. The game is suitable for general audiences.

Changes

If a future version ever collects anything, this page will be updated before that version ships, and the change will be described plainly here.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue on GitHub.

Ports Ahoy! is free, contains no ads, no in-app purchases, no energy meters and no paid speed-ups.
